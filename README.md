## About

I am a Research Fellow at **The University of Queensland**, working at the intersection of statistical learning, computational statistics, and forecasting. My research develops statistically principled and computationally efficient methods for learning from complex, incomplete, heterogeneous, and contaminated data.

My methodological work is motivated by applications in energy systems, environmental and geoscientific modelling, engineering, and other data-intensive scientific domains.

## Research Focus

- **Statistical learning under complex data conditions** — semi-supervised learning, informative missingness, finite mixture models, robust estimation, kernel methods, and regularisation.
- **Computational statistics and optimisation** — high-dimensional and non-convex optimisation, stochastic and iterative algorithms, and evolutionary and swarm-based methods.
- **Time series, forecasting, and causal inference** — probabilistic forecasting, uncertainty quantification, and data-driven modelling for energy, environmental, and socio-economic systems.

## Current Directions

- Likelihood-based semi-supervised learning with informative label missingness.
- Robust and adaptive support vector regression.
- Physics-informed and uncertainty-aware learning for environmental systems.
- Deterministic and probabilistic forecasting for energy and infrastructure.
- Trustworthy and responsible AI for scientific applications.
