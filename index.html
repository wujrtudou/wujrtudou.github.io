Research programme

Methods for complex data, built for consequential applications

Three connected themes shape my research—from statistical foundations to scalable computation and uncertainty-aware forecasting.

Research details
01

Statistical learning under complex data conditions

Semi-supervised inference, informative missingness, mixture models, robust estimation, kernel methods and regularisation.

  • Missing data
  • Robust learning
  • Mixture models
02

Computational statistics and optimisation

Efficient algorithms for high-dimensional and non-convex models, including stochastic, iterative, evolutionary and swarm-based methods.

  • Optimisation
  • Algorithms
  • High dimensions
03

Forecasting and causal dynamics

Probabilistic forecasting, uncertainty quantification and causal mechanisms in energy, environmental and socio-economic systems.

  • Time series
  • Uncertainty
  • Dynamics
